Famous Tourist Attractions

Great Wall of China

Walk along one of the world's most famous landmarks and discover China's incredible history.

Beijing

Explore China's capital, known for its historic palaces, temples, and traditional architecture.

Terracotta Army

The Terracotta Army is a famous historical attraction in Xi’an, China, featuring thousands of life-sized clay soldiers built to protect the tomb of China's first emperor, Qin Shi Huang.
